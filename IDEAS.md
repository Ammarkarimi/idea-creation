# Businesses you can run with (almost) only Claude

## Reality check: what "only Claude" means
Claude **can** produce: text (scripts, articles, books, emails), working code (websites, apps, extensions, games), and graphics drawn with code (SVG, charts, animation code). It can also produce documents (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX). In this environment it can deploy websites (Vercel connector) and draft email campaigns (Mailchimp connector).

Claude **cannot**:
- generate realistic images, photos, a voice or video footage by itself
- create accounts for you or pass identity checks
- take payments
- upload to YouTube

So the true minimum stack is **Claude + you (accounts, payouts, uploading, judgement) + free tools**. Free tools means things like ffmpeg, a free open-source text-to-speech (TTS) engine, Stripe/Gumroad and a domain.

## Is a tech YouTube channel possible? Yes, as a "faceless" channel, with caveats
- **Claude does:** research, scripts, titles and descriptions, and thumbnail layouts (as SVG). It also writes the code that *renders* the video: Manim or Remotion animations, code walkthroughs, terminal recordings and diagram animations, assembled into an MP4 with ffmpeg.
- **You add:** your own voice (best), or open-source TTS (e.g. Piper), or a text-and-music format with no voice. You also do the upload and the community replies.
- **Caveats:**
  - Getting paid by YouTube requires 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours. That usually takes 6–18 months.
  - YouTube's policy on "inauthentic" (mass-produced, repetitive) content demonetizes low-effort AI channels. Each video needs real insight and a human touch.
- **Verdict:** possible, but the slowest way to make money. It works best as a marketing channel for one of the products below.

## Recommendation
- **Fastest to first income:** #1 freelance services or #12 local-business automation. You sell your time, and Claude multiplies your output.
- **Best long-term leverage:** #2 micro-SaaS or #3/#4 digital products. Build once, sell many times.
- **Combined play:** run tech YouTube (#9) or a newsletter (#6) as the audience engine, and sell your own products (#2–#4) to that audience. That way you earn before YouTube monetization kicks in.
